## Ignem Spells

## 火法术 (Ignem Spells)

As fire is volatile and dangerous, so are these spells. Magi who pursue the Art of fire do so with fine regard for the dangers and powers involved, for fire is both dangerous and mighty, to friend and foe alike. Storyguides should make Ignem botches particularly deadly.

火焰反复无常而又危险，这些法术亦是如此。钻研火之秘艺的法师，必须充分认识其中蕴含的危险与力量，因为无论面对敌友，火焰都既危险又强大。指引者应让火法术的大失败格外致命。

Light and heat also fall within the domain of this Form. It's important to remember that heat also includes relative absence of heat, so spells that result in cold temperatures are possible through Ignem.

光与热也属于此型式的领域。需要谨记，热也包括热的相对缺失，因此火魔法同样可以产生低温效果。

The base Individual for Ignem is a large campfire or the fire in the hearth of a great hall. Fire damage is explained on page 407.

火型式的基础个体，是一堆大型营火，或大厅壁炉中的炉火。火焰伤害见第 407 页。

### Creo Ignem Guidelines

### 创生火准则 (Creo Ignem Guidelines)

Spells that create fires are versatile weapons — destructive to living things, undead, buildings, and morale. Those who depend on fire spells, however, risk botching. Fire is an unforgiving element for those who lose control of it, and self-incineration is always a possibility.

创造火焰的法术是用途广泛的武器，能够摧毁生物、亡灵与建筑，也能打击士气。然而，依赖火法术的人必须承担大失败的风险。火焰绝不会宽恕失去控制的人，施法者随时可能引火烧身。

Typical sigils seen in Creo Ignem spells involve the flame being a specific color or shape, or giving off an unusual smell.

创生火法术的典型法师印记，包括让火焰呈现特定颜色或形状，或散发异常气味。

### Muto Ignem Guidelines

### 万变火准则 (Muto Ignem Guidelines)

The intensity of a fire also affects the difficulty of changing it. For every five points by which the fire's damage exceeds +5, add one magnitude to the level of the spell.

火焰强度也会影响改变它的难度。火焰伤害每比 +5 高五点，法术等级便增加一个量级。
